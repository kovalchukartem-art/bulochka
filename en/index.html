Булочка

Play with your child in real life: a new idea every day for their age, the season and the place — and a cat who celebrates with you.

An app for parents and caregivers of children aged 0–8. The app is in Ukrainian. iPhone and iPad.

An idea for today

Over a thousand games for children from birth to 8 years — matched to age, season, place (home, city, village, outdoors) and your free time.

Step by step, safely

Every game comes with short steps, what to say to your child, how to make it easier or harder, and safety tips.

Bulochka the cat

A lively 3D cat celebrates your adventures: waves, jumps, meows, and earns rewards and outfits for every game.

Memories for your family only

Notes and photos from your games stay on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

A game is always led by an adult who stays with the child. You know your child best — change or skip an activity if it does not suit them.
