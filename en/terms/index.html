1. Responsibility for your child

You, the adult leading the game, are fully and personally responsible for the safety, health and supervision of the child during any activity, including activities suggested in the app.

You decide whether an activity suits your child, taking into account their age, health, allergies, developmental needs, mood, the weather and the place. If in doubt, change the game or skip it.

You supervise the child at all times — especially near water, food, small objects, animals, plants, roads, heights, heat and sharp objects — and follow the safety tips on the activity card.

The tips on the cards are general: they cannot take into account every circumstance of your family and the place where you play.

By using the app you agree that responsibility for carrying out the activities and their consequences lies with the adult leading them, not with the app or its team — to the extent permitted by law.

2. What Bulochka is

Bulochka suggests ideas for an adult and a child to play together in real life.

The app is not a medical, psychological, educational or rehabilitation service and does not replace advice from a doctor, psychologist or other professional.

3. Who uses the app

Only an adult creates the profile: parents, guardians or other adults caring for the child.

The child does not use the app alone: the game is led by an adult who stays nearby the whole time.

4. Ideas in the app

We prepare the ideas with care for safety, but we cannot guarantee that every activity suits every child and every situation. The ideas are provided “as is”.

The test version of the app contains ideas that are still being reviewed.

The free trial is offered once per Apple ID; any unused part of it is forfeited when you purchase a paid subscription.
