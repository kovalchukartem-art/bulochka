Privacy Policy

Version of 25 October 2026

1. In short

Everything you enter in Bulochka is stored only on your device. We have no servers that this data is sent to; we cannot see or read it.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no geolocation and no sharing of data with third parties.

2. Who is responsible for the app

Bulochka is developed by Artem Kovalchuk, a private individual. Questions about your data and this policy: efremovamarina069@gmail.com.

3. What data the app holds

About the adult: a name (optional) and a role — mum, dad, guardian or other.

About the child: a nickname (optional), date of birth and favourite topics — to suggest activities that fit the age.

Your history: activities you played, favourite ideas, rewards, the cat’s outfit, settings.

Family memories: text and photos that you add yourself. You choose photos from your library; the app copies the chosen photo into its own folder on the device and never sends it anywhere. The app does not ask for camera access.

4. Where the data is stored

Only in your device’s storage, inside the app. The app works offline and sends no personal data to us or anyone else.

If device backup is turned on (iCloud or a computer), iOS may include the app’s data in the device backup — this is controlled by Apple and by you; we have no access to those backups.

5. Purchases

You subscribe to Bulochka+ through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple ID e-mail.

The app only checks on the device (with Apple’s StoreKit) whether the subscription is active and until when, and stores that flag locally. It is not sent anywhere.

6. Children

The app is used by an adult, who creates the child’s profile and plays together with the child. The adult enters the child’s data, and it never leaves the device.

We deliberately do not collect data about children and never show ads to children.

7. Your control

You can view, change, export or delete all data at any time: Settings → My data. Deleting the app also deletes all its data from the device.

Because the data stays with you, you exercise all your rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) directly in the app. Questions: efremovamarina069@gmail.com.

8. Changes

If we ever add a feature that sends data off the device (for example, syncing between phones), we will update this policy in advance, describe exactly what is sent and where, and ask for your consent in the app.
