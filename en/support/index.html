FAQ

Do I need internet or an account?

No. The app works offline. You create a profile only on your phone — no email or password.

Where are my data and photos stored?

Only on your device. We receive no data at all. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete all data?

Settings → My data → Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes all its data.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

App data moves with your iPhone backup (iCloud, a computer or Quick Start). You can also save a copy: Settings → My data → Export data.

How do I turn sounds off?

Settings → Motion and feel → Bulochka sounds: off, quiet or normal. The iPhone Silent switch also mutes them.

What ages are the games for?

From birth to 8 years. The app only shows games that fit the age of the child in the profile.

How much does it cost?

The download is free. 3 new games are free every day, games you have played stay available, and the cat, rewards and memories are free too. Bulochka+ (monthly or yearly, first 7 days free) unlocks all games with no limit and the cat’s whole wardrobe.

How do I cancel Bulochka+ or restore a purchase?

To cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bulochka (or in the app: Settings → Bulochka+ → Manage subscription). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. On a new phone: Bulochka+ screen → Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Who is responsible for safety during a game?

The adult leading the game. Every card has safety tips and signs that it is time to stop. See the Terms of Use.

Found a mistake or a game looks unsafe?

Write to efremovamarina069@gmail.com with the game’s name and what is wrong. We reply and fix it in updates.
